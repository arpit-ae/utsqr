Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Maintained by Kanish

1. About this directory

Kanish Station QR is an independent, unofficial station QR directory. You can browse its published entries without creating an account.

2. Browser storage

The directory saves favorites locally in your browser using localStorage. You can remove favorites within the site or clear your browser's stored data. The site may cache pages and selected QR images to support faster repeat access.

3. Hosting and external services

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which may process technical request information to deliver and protect the service. The homepage also requests fonts from Google Fonts. External websites, including app stores and GitHub, operate under their own privacy policies. This version of the site does not include the previous Vercel analytics script.

4. Contributions and messages

Missing-QR submissions and contact requests open GitHub Issues. Anything posted in a public issue may be visible to others. Avoid sharing private information, ID numbers or confidential documents in public issues.

5. Contact

For privacy or content concerns, contact Kanish through the project's GitHub Issues.
